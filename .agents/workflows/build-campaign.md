---
title: Build Job Outreach Campaign
description: Research 100 safe leads, generate emails, and stop at the five-email approval gate.
---

1. Read the project rules and CV.
2. Initialize the database if needed.
3. Research and score companies using the configured ICP.
4. Classify companies into OUTREACH / WATCHLIST / REJECT.
5. Research up to 3 contacts per OUTREACH company using configured priority.
6. Resolve honorific; quarantine uncertain cases.
7. Find and verify professional email addresses.
8. Generate evidence-backed 1–2 sentence personalization.
9. Render the locked email template and require CV attachment.
10. Build/freeze up to 100 eligible leads.
11. Generate Excel export and five-email review preview.
12. Present the five examples and STOP for explicit approval. Do not schedule or send.
